# What's changed

This page lists the changes to the report Analysis: Dog Poop Complaints in Manhattan that a reader would notice, newest first, including corrections when we published something wrong.

- **History begins:** 2026-04-07, the day the report was first published. Dates are the day each version went up on the site, in New York time. Corrections made before this date may be missing.
- **Data refresh:** only when the report is rebuilt. Each rebuild pulls 311 service requests and other City data from NYC Open Data again, so counts can change from one version to the next.

To report a bug in the report, or to ask for a new feature, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/issues).

## 2026-11-12: A page that lists what changed in the report

- **Tags:** Site
- **What changed:** This page is new. It lists each version of the report and any correction, newest first. The report links to it from its footer the next time the report is rebuilt.
- **More detail:** [The file this page is built from](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/blob/main/WHATS-CHANGED.md)

## 2026-05-21: Rebuilt with 311 data through May 19, 2026

- **Tags:** Data
- **What changed:** The report was rebuilt with newer 311 data, so several counts changed. For example, the count of dog poop service requests in Manhattan since 2020 went from 2,552 (through April 29, 2026) to 2,581 (through May 19, 2026).
- **More detail:** [The commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/commit/11f7438)

## 2026-05-01: Citywide numbers for the February 2026 storms

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** The report now opens with a citywide comparison: New Yorkers filed 380 dog poop complaints in February 2025 and 1,284 in February 2026, after two winter storms. It also shows, month by month since 2020, the share of all Manhattan 311 requests that are about dog poop. Counts were refreshed with data through April 29, 2026.
- **More detail:** [The commits from May 1, 2026](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/commits/main/?since=2026-05-01&until=2026-05-02)

## 2026-04-30: The example public message was taken out

- **Tags:** Site
- **What changed:** The report had ended with an example of a message for a public awareness campaign, written from its numbers. That example was taken out. Nothing else in the report changed.
- **More detail:** [The commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/commit/3b03dda)

## 2026-04-22: Hot spots across Manhattan, and where the bins are

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** The report now finds the blocks with the most dog poop complaints in each of Manhattan's 12 community districts since January 1, 2024. Its maps add the 25 dog waste bag dispensers and the DSNY litter baskets in Manhattan, plus privately owned public spaces and dog runs. The chart of complaints by month, left out of the April 15 version, is back.
- **More detail:** [The commits from April 22, 2026](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/commits/main/?since=2026-04-22&until=2026-04-23)

## 2026-04-15: Districts compared with their overall 311 volume

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** A second version compares each Manhattan community district's dog poop complaints with what its overall number of 311 requests would predict. Manhattan Community District 12 has more than expected, with a cluster on and around Riverside Drive between West 158th and West 160th streets. The earlier charts comparing complaints with LinkNYC kiosks, dog licenses and rodent complaints were taken out.
- **More detail:** [The commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/commit/9d61d01)

## 2026-04-08: Comparisons with kiosks, dog licenses and rodent complaints

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** The report added counts of 2026 complaints near LinkNYC kiosks with screens, and compared complaints by ZIP code with dog licenses and with rodent complaints.
- **More detail:** [The commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/commit/d1ce143)

## 2026-04-07: The report is published

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** The first version names the six 311 request types about dog poop and counts 2,510 of them in Manhattan between January 3, 2020 and April 5, 2026, with a chart of complaints by month, a table of 2026 complaints by community board, and a map.
- **More detail:** [The commit](https://github.com/BetaNYC/mbpo-dog-poop/commit/acee3fb)
